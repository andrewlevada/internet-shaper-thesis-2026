Rain Delays Qualifying For The Indy 500

Qualifying for the Indianapolis 500 was rained out Saturday and was pushed back to Sunday.

ByMark Smith•
May 17, 2026Updated: May 17, 2026, 9:23 am EDTPublished: May 16, 2026, 10:19 am EDT


The first round of qualifying for the 110th Indianapolis 500 was rained out Saturday, leading to a busy, condensed schedule on Sunday.

Storms and showers rained out practice on Saturday morning and kept coming into the afternoon, forcing IndyCar to push all qualifying to Sunday.


Qualifying will begin at noon on Sunday, and each driver will only get one try at qualifying for the Fast 12 and a second qualifying session. More on this procedure can be found here.

Fortunately for IndyCar, Sunday's activities at Indianapolis Motor Speedway won't be rained out. Forecasters are calling for a dry, warm and mostly sunny day on Sunday.

The green flag will drop on the iconic race at 12:45 p.m. on May 24.
