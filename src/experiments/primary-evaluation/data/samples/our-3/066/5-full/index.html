I want a feature or extension turned on

Some of Fandom's features can be enabled and disabled by local admins using Admin Dashboard on that wiki, such as:

  • Requiring all contributors to log in
  • Message Wall
  • Article Comments
  • Blogs

If you are an admin, click on "Admin" on your bottom toolbar to find this page.

 

To request an interlanguage link please see this help desk article.

 

For other features and extensions, such as the following, you may need assistance:

  • Turning on custom JavaScript editing on for your wiki.
    • Note: ImportJS does not require this to be enabled.
  • Enabling achievements (aka badges).
  • Other extensions listed here, such as DPL or Variables.

If your wiki has Fandom team contact assigned, please ask them for assistance. Otherwise, click here to contact us for these extensions.
